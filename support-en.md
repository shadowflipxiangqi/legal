# Support

## Contact

**Email: ShadowFlipService@163.com**

This is currently the only support channel. The app is built and maintained by an
independent solo developer. **I reply within 3 business days.** If you haven't heard
back after 5 business days, please send your message again — on rare occasions email
gets filtered out.

To help resolve your issue faster, please include:

- Device model and iOS version (e.g. iPhone 15 Pro / iOS 18.2)
- App version (found under "Profile → About")
- What happened, and the steps to reproduce it
- A screenshot or screen recording, if convenient

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Purchases and Unlocks

**My purchases are missing on a new device.**
Open "Shop → Modes" and tap **Restore Purchases** at the bottom of the page.
Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so signing in with the same Apple ID restores
everything you own.

**Restore Purchases didn't unlock anything.**
Make sure the device is signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase and
that you have a working network connection, then restart the app and try again.
If it still fails, contact us and include your receipt (the App Store purchase email
is fine).

**Are in-app purchases one-time or subscriptions?**
All purchases are **one-time**. Nothing auto-renews and you will not be charged again.

**Can I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled entirely by Apple; developers cannot process them.
Please submit a request at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### Account and Data

**Do I need an account to play?**
No. Choose **Continue as Guest** to access all offline features, including every game
mode and AI matches. Signing in with your Apple ID and enabling Game Center is only
required for online play, global leaderboards, and achievements.

**Will I lose my progress when I switch devices?**
No. Rank, match records, and achievement progress sync automatically through iCloud.
Sign in with the same Apple ID and enable iCloud on both devices. Without iCloud,
data stays on the device only.

**How do I delete my data?**
Deleting the app removes all local data. iCloud data can be removed under
"Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage".
Contact us if you need help.

### Gameplay

**I don't understand the rules.**
Tap the **book icon** at the top-left of the lobby for the full rulebook, including a
beginner walkthrough.

**Online matchmaking can't find an opponent.**
Make sure you're signed in to Game Center with a working connection. The player pool
is small right after launch — try a different time of day, or play against the AI.

**The AI is too strong / too weak.**
Difficulty can be adjusted in the game setup screen. Challenge mode offers finer tiers.
